2. Description of Service

SwipeLanguage is a multi-language learning mobile application that helps users learn vocabulary through swipe-based flashcard exercises, a Match Game, daily challenges, and progress tracking. The app supports 10 languages (English, Czech, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Dutch, and Polish) with 20+ bidirectional language pairs.
